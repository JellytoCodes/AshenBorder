SAVE MYSELF: ASHEN BORDER - Original ruined outpost assets
Original procedural geometry and palette authored by this project in
Scripts/BuildAshenOutpost.py. No external model or texture bytes are copied.
The terrain heightfield is the project's preserved LF05 heightmap; its vertex,
normal, UV and index bytes are unchanged. Only its two texture images change.
These stylized props suggest a fictional late-17th-century border outpost;
they are not historical replicas and do not add collision or game rules.
Project-authored source and generated files may be stored, modified and
redistributed with this project's repository and builds. Preserve this notice
with redistributed bundles. No unrelated dependency license is changed.
